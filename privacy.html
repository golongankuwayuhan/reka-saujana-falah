3. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • License & Order Data: Customer email address and name (relayed securely by our Merchant of Record) to generate, dispatch, and manage digital license keys.
  • Hardware Verification Hash: A one-way cryptographic hash of your Mac's hardware identifier (UUID) collected during license activation. This hash contains zero personally identifiable information and serves solely to enforce your purchased device quota (e.g. 1 Mac on Plus, 3 Macs on Pro, 5 Macs on Power Pack).
  • Direct Communications: Any inquiries, contact requests, or support emails you send to our official communication desks.
  • Web Telemetry: Standard non-identifying server logs for security defense and rate-limiting.

4. Role of Merchant of Record (Paddle.com)

When purchasing LuminaTweak Glass Edition, your commercial checkout is processed by an authorized Merchant of Record (Paddle.com):

  • The Merchant of Record (Paddle.com Market Ltd) acts as an independent Data Controller regarding payment transactions, payment card details, billing addresses, and local tax remittances.
  • The MoR maintains PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance and processes financial information in full accordance with its global privacy policy.

5. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR, CCPA & Indonesian Law)

We process your data under the following legal frameworks:

  • Performance of a Contract: Generating license keys, validating device seats, and fulfilling software delivery.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: Tax documentation, trade compliance, and regulatory D-U-N-S verification.
  • Legitimate Interests: Preventing unauthorized software cracking, duplicate seat fraud, and maintaining system integrity.
  • Applicable Statutes: EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Indonesian Personal Data Protection Act (UU PDP No. 27/2022).
