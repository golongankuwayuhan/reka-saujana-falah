Reka Saujana Falah provides a comprehensive 30-calendar-day refund window starting from the timestamp of purchase for all LuminaTweak Glass Edition tiers (Plus $39, Pro $59, and Power Pack $99).

Any refund request submitted within this 30-day window will be honored promptly without tedious bureaucratic friction.

2. Eligibility & Acceptable Refund Grounds

You are entitled to a full refund for any valid reason, including:

  • Hardware/OS Incompatibility: The utility fails to perform correctly on your specific macOS build or hardware configuration (although we support macOS Monterey through macOS Sequoia across Apple Silicon M1-M4 and Intel chips).
  • Feature Performance Discrepancy: A feature or the 1:1 Live Simulator does not match the specifications documented on our website.
  • Accidental Duplicate Order: You inadvertently purchased multiple licenses for the same tier.
  • General Dissatisfaction: The workflow or customizer does not suit your personal aesthetic or productivity habits.
