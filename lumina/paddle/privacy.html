3. Data yang Kami Kumpulkan

Kami membatasi pengumpulan data pada hal-hal yang esensial secara teknis:

  • Data Transaksi MoR: Nomor pesanan, tanggal transaksi, dan alamat email pembeli yang dikirimkan oleh Paddle untuk penerbitan lisensi.

4. Peran Merchant of Record (Paddle.com)

Paddle.com Market Ltd bertindak sebagai Pengendali Data independen untuk rincian kartu pembayaran, alamat penagihan, dan administrasi pajak internasional di bawah standar PCI-DSS Level 1.

5. Dasar Hukum Pemrosesan Data

Pemrosesan data didasarkan pada pemenuhan kontrak lisensi perangkat lunak, kewajiban hukum perpajakan, dan asas UU Perlindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP No. 27/2022) serta GDPR/CCPA.

6. Tujuan Pemrosesan & Pemanfaatan Data

Data hanya digunakan untuk:

  • Memvalidasi keabsahan lisensi aplikasi LuminaTweak Glass saat pertama kali dibuka.
  • Menyediakan pembaruan pemeliharaan perangkat lunak berkala dan bantuan aktivasi lisensi.
  • Memfasilitasi migrasi atau pemindahan lisensi ke Mac baru.

1. Introduction & Privacy Commitment

Reka Saujana Falah ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy details our data practices when you purchase and use LuminaTweak Glass Edition via Paddle.com.

2. Data Controller Identification

Legal Entity: Reka Saujana Falah
Domicile: West Java, Indonesia
Privacy Desk Email: admin@rekasaujanafalah.com

3. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization principles:

  • Merchant of Record Data: Order reference number, date of purchase, and customer email address transmitted by Paddle for license delivery.
  • Device Activation Hash: An anonymous, one-way cryptographic hash of your Mac device UUID during license validation. This hash cannot identify your physical identity and is used strictly to enforce license seat quotas (1 Mac, 3 Macs, or 5 Macs).
  • Support Correspondence: Communications sent to our official email desk for technical assistance.

4. Role of Merchant of Record (Paddle.com)

Paddle.com Market Ltd operates as an independent Data Controller for customer payment information, billing address, and global sales tax remittances. Paddle adheres to PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance and international data security standards.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Data processing is conducted on the grounds of contractual necessity (generating and validating your software license), legal obligation compliance, and adherence to EU GDPR, California CCPA, and Indonesian UU PDP No. 27/2022.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Validate software license validity upon launch.
  • Deliver software maintenance releases and license activation assistance.
  • Facilitate license transfers when replacing or upgrading your Mac.
  • Process refund requests and deactivate revoked license keys via Paddle.

7. Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances. Data is shared solely with Paddle.com (payment clearing) and Zoho Mail (official correspondence).

8. Data Security & Storage

All network communications utilize industry-standard TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. License data is retained during the active lifetime of your license.

9. User Rights & Regulatory Protections

You have the right to request access to your activation records, update your email address, or request permanent anonymization/erasure of your activation record upon license termination.

10. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses client-side localStorage solely to remember your preferred interface language (EN or ID). We use zero invasive third-party tracking cookies.

11. Children's Privacy Protection

Our software utilities are designed for general developers and computer users. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under the age of 13.
